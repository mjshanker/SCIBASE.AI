# Requirements Map

## Structured peer reviews

- Adds pre-review assignment screening for public, semi-private, and anonymous review modes.
- Keeps reviewer packets anonymized so conflict stewardship does not expose blind-review identities.

## Review history and reviewer profiles

- Uses reviewer completion count, calibration score, and disclosure freshness before allowing reputation-bearing reviews.
- Produces audit digests that can be attached to project timelines and reviewer history.

## Contributor credit and reputation scoring

- Prevents conflicted reviews from creating review credit or reputation impact.
- Quantifies `reputationWeightAtRisk` so maintainers can see how much leaderboard/profile exposure is protected.

## Leaderboards and badges

- Flags high-reputation-weight review assignments before they influence public project pages, profile badges, or leaderboards.

## Anonymous review modes

- Detects double-blind institution overlap and prior public project contact as blind-review integrity risks.
